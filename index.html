Support

Something broken, confusing, or missing? Email maximilian.michlits@gmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within a few days.

It helps to include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The version of Bloom, shown on its App Store page
  • What you did, and what happened instead

Lost your forest? Bloom stores everything on your device, so deleting the app deletes your sessions. Your forest is included in your iPhone backup, though, so restoring a backup brings it back.

Lost your coffee tree? It comes back on its own when you use the same Apple ID. If it does not, open Buy me a coffee and tap Restore purchases.

Lost your profile or friend code? A profile belongs to the copy of Bloom on your phone, so reinstalling the app starts a new one with a new code. Make a new profile under Menu › Profile and share the new code with your friends.

Privacy Policy

Bloom is a focus timer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version: Bloom collects nothing unless you choose a name. There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app remembers about your focus sessions is stored on your own device. The one optional feature that uses a server, planting together with a friend, is described below, and it only ever sends the name and code you chose.

What Bloom stores on your device

Bloom saves the following locally, so the app still knows what you were doing after you close it:

  • Your focus sessions — when each one finished, how long it lasted, which tree you grew, and whether you completed or abandoned it.
  • The tree you have selected, and which trees you have unlocked through your total study time.
  • Whether you have bought the coffee that unlocks the coffee tree.
  • Your settings — sound and finish-chime preferences, whether session notifications are enabled, your week-start, day-start and time-zone preferences, and your chosen language.
  • A session in progress, so that a running timer survives closing or restarting the app.

All of this is written to your device’s private app storage. It is never transmitted anywhere, and no one but you can read it.

Planting together, and the optional profile

Bloom can run a focus session together with a friend: you both start the same countdown, and if either of you gives up, both trees die. For two phones to find each other, something has to sit between them, so this feature uses a server.

You create a profile only if you ask to. The first time you open Plant together or Profile in the menu, Bloom asks for a name. Until you enter one, the app makes no network connection at all. If you never do, nothing in this section applies to you.

A profile consists of:

  • A display name you type. It is shown to the friend you plant with.
  • A six-character code, generated for you, which a friend types to join you. It is not derived from anything about you.
  • A random identifier that the server assigns to your copy of the app, so it can tell your profile from everyone else’s. It is not your Apple ID, your email, your phone number or anything else about you, none of which Bloom ever asks for.
  • A notification token, only if you have session notifications turned on, so that the server can tell your phone when your friend starts or gives up while your screen is locked. It is removed if you turn notifications off.

While you plant together, the server also keeps the shared session: who the two of you are, how long it runs, when it started and whether one of you gave up. It does not receive your other sessions, your forest, your settings or your labels; those stay on your device as before.

The server is hosted by Supabase in the European Union. Supabase processes this data on our behalf under Supabase’s privacy policy. Push notifications are delivered through Expo’s push service and Apple’s notification service, which see the message text and your notification token and nothing else.

Your profile is tied to the copy of Bloom on your phone. Deleting the app abandons it, and reinstalling starts a new one with a new code.

What Bloom does not do

  • It does not ask for your email, phone number, Apple ID or any other personal detail. A profile is only a name you choose.
  • It does not contain analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • It never sends your focus sessions, your forest or your settings anywhere. Everything except buying the coffee and planting together works offline.

Notifications

If you turn on session notifications, Bloom asks iOS for permission to send them. The end-of-session reminder is a local notification: your device schedules and delivers it itself, with no server involved.

If you also plant together with a friend, the server sends a push notification when your friend starts the session or gives up, so you learn of it even with the phone locked. These are the only push notifications Bloom sends, and they stop if you turn session notifications off, delete your profile or never create one.

Children

Bloom is suitable for all ages. It collects no information from anyone, including children, beyond the chosen display name described above.

Buying a coffee

The one purchase in Bloom, Buy me a coffee, is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Bloom never sees your payment details or your Apple ID. It only asks the App Store whether the coffee has been bought, so that the coffee tree comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone.

Deleting your data

On your device: deleting Bloom removes everything it has stored there, permanently, apart from the copy in your own iPhone backups. A coffee you bought stays with your Apple ID.

On the server: open Menu › Profile › Delete profile. This removes your name, your code, your identifier, your notification token and any shared sessions you hosted from the server immediately. You can also write to the address below and we will do the same for you. Server backups are kept for up to 90 days and then discarded.

Information Apple receives

Bloom is distributed through the App Store. Apple collects its own information about downloads and purchases under Apple’s privacy policy, which is outside our control and unrelated to anything the app itself does.

Changes to this policy

If Bloom gains a feature that changes any of the above — for example a friends list — this policy will be updated before that feature is released, and the date at the top will change.
